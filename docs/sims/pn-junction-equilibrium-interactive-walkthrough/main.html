Challenge Questions

1. Which concept in this walkthrough directly explains why heavier doping narrows the depletion region on that side?

Correct. Because NA·xp = ND·xn, a larger NA forces a smaller xp to keep the exposed charge balanced with the opposite side.

2. Which quantity in this chapter changes once an applied bias is introduced in Chapter 15?

Correct. Applied bias effectively replaces Vbi with Vbi minus (forward) or plus (reverse) the applied voltage inside the W formula, changing W and therefore Cj — exactly what Chapter 15 formalizes.
