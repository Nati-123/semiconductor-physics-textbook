<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots a simplified sigmoid model of the dopant ionization fraction, \(f_{ion}(T)=1/(1+B\,e^{E/k_BT})\), against temperature, with numeric axis ticks on both axes. A movable temperature marker reads off the exact fraction at any point, and a companion energy-level diagram shows E_C and the donor level E_D (or E_V and the acceptor level E_A) with a handful of representative carriers that visibly hop from bound to free as the ionized fraction rises — connecting the curve directly to the physical picture.
</p>
