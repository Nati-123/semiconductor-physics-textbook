<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim shows two side-by-side panels: a <strong>bipolar transistor</strong>'s collector current \(I_C=\beta I_B\), linear in base current, and a <strong>MOSFET</strong>'s drain current \(I_D=(\mu_nC_{ox}/2)(W/L)(V_{GS}-V_T)^2\), quadratic in gate overdrive voltage — directly contrasting the two devices' fundamentally different control relationships.
</p>
