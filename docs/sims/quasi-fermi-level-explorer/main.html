Guided Exploration & Challenge Questions

Try This

  • Try this: Start with Δn = 0 and confirm E_Fn and E_Fp sit at the same level (the equilibrium Fermi level). Then raise Δn and watch them split apart.
  • Predict: Before switching doping type, predict which quasi-Fermi level (E_Fn or E_Fp) will move the most as Δn increases in n-type material.
  • Observe: In n-type material, E_Fn barely moves (n is already large) while E_Fp swings dramatically toward midgap, since p is the minority carrier and is most strongly perturbed by injection.

Connects to Chapter 13: Under illumination or carrier injection, electrons and holes are no longer in mutual equilibrium, so a single Fermi level can no longer describe both populations. Instead, separate quasi-Fermi levels \(E_{Fn}\) and \(E_{Fp}\) describe each carrier population's occupation statistics, splitting apart from the equilibrium Fermi level by an amount that grows with the injection level.

What Each Control Does

Doping type
Switches between n-type and p-type silicon, changing which carrier starts as majority.
Doping concentration N
Sets the equilibrium majority carrier concentration.
Δn (injection level)
Sets the excess carrier concentration added equally to both n and p, splitting the quasi-Fermi levels.

Challenge Questions

1. At Δn = 0 (no injection), where do E_Fn and E_Fp sit?

At equilibrium there is only one Fermi level; the quasi-Fermi levels reduce to E_Fn=E_Fp=E_F when Δn=0.
