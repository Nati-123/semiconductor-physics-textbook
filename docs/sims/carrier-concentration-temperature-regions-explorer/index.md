<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots a doped silicon sample's free carrier concentration against temperature using the simplified combination \(n(T)\approx N_D f_{ion}(T) + n_i(T)\), and shades the plot into the three characteristic temperature regions: freeze-out (blue), extrinsic/saturation (green), and intrinsic (red). Adjustable donor concentration and ionization energy sliders let students see directly how doping level and dopant species shift each region's boundaries.
</p>
