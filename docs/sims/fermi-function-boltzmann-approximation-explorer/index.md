<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots the exact Fermi-Dirac function, \(f(E)=1/[1+e^{(E-E_F)/k_BT}]\), alongside its Boltzmann approximation, \(f(E)\approx e^{-(E-E_F)/k_BT}\), and shades the energy range where the two agree to within 5%. A temperature slider rescales both curves, and a movable marker reads off the exact numeric percent difference at any energy.
</p>
