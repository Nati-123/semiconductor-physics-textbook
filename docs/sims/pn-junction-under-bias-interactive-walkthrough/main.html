Challenge Questions

1. Which concept in this walkthrough directly sets the boundary condition used to solve for the injected carrier profile?

Correct. The law of the junction, pn(xn)=pn0·e^(V/VT), is the boundary condition that launches the continuity-equation solution for the injected carrier profile.

Revisit Step 2 — which concept connects applied voltage to the carrier concentration right at the depletion edge?

2. Which breakdown mechanism dominates in heavily-doped junctions with narrow depletion regions?

Correct. Zener breakdown is quantum tunneling through a thin barrier, which only becomes significant when the depletion region is narrow — true for heavily-doped junctions.
