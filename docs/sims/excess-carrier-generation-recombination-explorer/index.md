<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots excess minority carrier concentration \(\Delta n(t)\) and \(\Delta p(t)\) under student-controlled generation: a real Start/Stop toggle lets you turn generation on (approach toward a steady state \(\Delta n_{ss}=G\tau\), derived directly from the generation rate \(G\) and lifetime \(\tau\)) or off (exponential decay, \(\Delta n(t)=\Delta n(T_{on})e^{-(t-T_{on})/\tau}\), starting from whenever you choose to stop generation). One full lifetime is marked directly on the decay curve. A movable time marker reads off the instantaneous excess concentration, recombination rate \(R=\Delta n/\tau\), and a preview of the injection-level ratio \(\Delta n/N\) covered in the next section.
</p>
