<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim holds donor and acceptor concentration fixed and sweeps temperature from 150 K to 800 K, showing \(E_F\) on a band diagram alongside a trend chart of \(E_F-E_i\) across the whole range. As \(n_i(T)\) grows exponentially with temperature, \(E_F\) drifts from its doping-set position at low-to-moderate T toward \(E_i\) at high T — the exact-equation version of Chapter 8's freeze-out/extrinsic/intrinsic temperature story.
</p>
