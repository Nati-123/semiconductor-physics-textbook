<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots the estimated avalanche breakdown voltage \(V_{BR}\approx\varepsilon E_{crit}^2/(2qN)\) against lightly-doped-side concentration \(N\) on a log-log chart with numeric axis tick labels and light red/green shading for the approximate Zener-territory and avalanche-dominant regions, plus a dashed reference line at the classic ~6 V crossover (explicitly labeled as an approximate guide, not a sharp threshold). Side-by-side schematic panels compare the two <strong>reverse breakdown</strong> mechanisms with concrete labeled physical rows — doping level, depletion width, electric field strength, and dominant process — plus a gently looping animation of avalanche carrier multiplication and Zener tunneling, highlighting whichever mechanism is more likely at the current doping level. A readout below the chart also shows an order-of-magnitude estimate of the depletion width at breakdown.
</p>
