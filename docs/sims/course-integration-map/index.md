<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim shows how all 20 chapters of this course connect and build upon each other, as a force-directed graph. It is a deliberately high-level, chapter-level roadmap — separate from the 256-concept <a href="../../learning-graph/index.md" style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 600;">Learning Graph</a> — meant to be understood in a few seconds. Every arrow corresponds to a real "builds on" relationship stated in that chapter's own Prerequisites section. Hover a chapter for its title and a short description, click to highlight its prerequisite (blue) and dependent (orange) connections, drag nodes to explore, scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan, and double-click a chapter to open it directly.
</p>
