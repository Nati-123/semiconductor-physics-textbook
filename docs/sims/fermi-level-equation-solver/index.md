<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim is an equation-practice tool: choose what is known — doping (N_D, N_A), electron concentration n₀, hole concentration p₀, or Fermi level position — and the solver shows the relevant equation, substitutes the current numbers into it step by step, and plots the result on a band diagram. Unlike the other Chapter 10 and Chapter 9 MicroSims, which emphasize continuous drag-and-observe exploration, this one emphasizes the algebra: which equation applies, in what order, given what is already known.
</p>
