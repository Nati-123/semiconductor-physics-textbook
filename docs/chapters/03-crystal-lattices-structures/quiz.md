<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75;">What is a crystal lattice?</p>

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. A single atom bonded to its nearest neighbors
2. An infinite, perfectly periodic array of points in space, with no atoms attached
3. Any solid material with a fixed volume
4. The chemical formula of a compound
</div>

<details style="margin-top: 1rem;">
<summary style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; cursor: pointer;">Show Answer</summary>
<div style="background: #E7F7E7; border: 2px solid #81C784; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px 22px; margin-top: 10px;">
<p style="color: #2E7D32; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 0;">Correct Answer: B</p>
<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75; margin-bottom: 0;">A crystal lattice is a purely geometric idealization: an infinite, perfectly periodic array of points, with every point having an environment identical to every other point. Attaching a basis of atoms to each point produces a crystal structure.</p>

<p style="color: #1565C0; font-weight: 700; font-size: 1.08rem; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 14px;">Question 3</p>

<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75;">What is a unit cell?</p>

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. A single isolated atom

3. The energy required to remove an electron from an atom
4. A measure of electrical conductivity

<details style="margin-top: 1rem;">
<summary style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; cursor: pointer;">Show Answer</summary>
<div style="background: #E7F7E7; border: 2px solid #81C784; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px 22px; margin-top: 10px;">
<p style="color: #2E7D32; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 0;">Correct Answer: B</p>
<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75; margin-bottom: 0;">A unit cell is a repeating volume that, when tiled by simple translation, reproduces the full lattice. The conventional choice for a cubic crystal is a cube with edge length equal to the lattice constant.</p>
<p style="color: #555; font-style: italic; margin-bottom: 0; margin-top: 8px;"><strong>Concept Tested:</strong> Unit Cell</p>

<p style="color: #1565C0; font-weight: 700; font-size: 1.08rem; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 14px;">Question 13</p>

<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75;">Why is the coordination number of the diamond lattice structure 4 rather than 12?</p>

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Because diamond is not periodic
2. Because the large <span class="arithmatex">\((1/4,1/4,1/4)a\)</span> offset between the two interpenetrating FCC sublattices makes each atom's nearest neighbors the four closest atoms on the other sublattice, not the twelve neighbors within its own sublattice
3. Because diamond has no basis
4. Because diamond atoms carry no charge
</div>

<details style="margin-top: 1rem;">
<summary style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; cursor: pointer;">Show Answer</summary>
<div style="background: #E7F7E7; border: 2px solid #81C784; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px 22px; margin-top: 10px;">
<p style="color: #2E7D32; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 0;">Correct Answer: B</p>
<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75; margin-bottom: 0;">The offset between the two FCC sublattices is much larger than the spacing between nearest neighbors within a single FCC lattice, so each atom's true nearest neighbors are the four closest atoms on the other, shifted sublattice — giving tetrahedral, 4-fold coordination rather than the 12-fold coordination of a simple FCC lattice.</p>
<p style="color: #555; font-style: italic; margin-bottom: 0; margin-top: 8px;"><strong>Concept Tested:</strong> Diamond Lattice Structure</p>

<p style="color: #1565C0; font-weight: 700; font-size: 1.08rem; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 14px;">Question 15</p>

<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75;">Why does the zincblende structure lack the inversion symmetry present in the diamond structure?</p>

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Because inverting through an atom would map its neighbors onto positions occupied by a different, non-interchangeable atomic species
2. Because zincblende crystals are not periodic
3. Because zincblende has a smaller lattice constant
4. Because zincblende atoms are not tetrahedrally coordinated
</div>

<details style="margin-top: 1rem;">
<summary style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; cursor: pointer;">Show Answer</summary>
<div style="background: #E7F7E7; border: 2px solid #81C784; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px 22px; margin-top: 10px;">
<p style="color: #2E7D32; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 0;">Correct Answer: A</p>
<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75; margin-bottom: 0;">In diamond, every atom is chemically identical, so inversion maps the crystal onto itself. In zincblende, the two sublattices hold different species, so inverting through one atom maps its neighbors onto positions belonging to the other species — the two are not interchangeable, breaking inversion symmetry.</p>
<p style="color: #555; font-style: italic; margin-bottom: 0; margin-top: 8px;"><strong>Concept Tested:</strong> Zincblende Structure</p>

<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75;">A crystal plane intercepts the <span class="arithmatex">\(x\)</span> axis at <span class="arithmatex">\(2a\)</span>, the <span class="arithmatex">\(y\)</span> axis at <span class="arithmatex">\(1a\)</span>, and never intercepts the <span class="arithmatex">\(z\)</span> axis. What are its Miller indices?</p>

<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75; margin-bottom: 0;">A Miller index of 0 arises from an intercept at infinity, since <span class="arithmatex">\(1/\infty = 0\)</span>. Physically, this means the plane runs parallel to that axis and never crosses it.</p>
