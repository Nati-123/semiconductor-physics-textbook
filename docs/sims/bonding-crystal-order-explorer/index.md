<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim has two linked views, selected by a top-level dropdown. The <strong>Bond Types</strong> view schematically compares covalent bonding (a shared electron pair between two atoms), ionic bonding (an electron transferred completely between a cation and an anion, held together by Coulomb attraction), and metallic bonding (valence electrons delocalized into a mobile "electron sea" surrounding fixed positive ion cores). The <strong>Crystal Order</strong> view compares a perfectly periodic single crystal, a polycrystalline mosaic of small, differently-oriented grains separated by grain boundaries, and an amorphous arrangement with no long-range order at all — with an optional toggle that overlays vacancy, interstitial, and substitutional point defects onto the single-crystal grid.
</p>

<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">How to Use</h2>

<ol style="padding-left: 1.2rem; margin: 0.8rem 0 1.2rem 0;">
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.6rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;"><strong style="color: #5A3EED;">Select "Bond Types"</strong> and cycle through Covalent, Ionic, and Metallic</li>
