<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim charts measured ionization energies (in meV) for the most common donor dopants (P, As, Sb) and acceptor dopants (B, Al, Ga, In) in either silicon or germanium. Placing these real values next to the room-temperature thermal energy \(k_BT\approx26\) meV shows directly why nearly every dopant atom is ionized at room temperature.
</p>
