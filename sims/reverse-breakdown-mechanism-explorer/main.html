Connects to Chapter 15: Reverse breakdown occurs via two distinct mechanisms. Avalanche breakdown is an impact-ionization chain reaction that dominates in lightly-doped junctions with wide depletion regions, where carriers have room to accelerate to the critical field before colliding. Zener breakdown is quantum-mechanical tunneling that dominates in heavily-doped junctions with narrow depletion regions — typically in an approximate crossover region around 5-6 V in silicon, not a sharp universal threshold; real devices transition gradually between the two mechanisms.

Sets the lightly-doped side concentration, which sets the estimated avalanche breakdown voltage \(V_{BR}\propto1/N\) and the estimated one-sided depletion width \(W\) shown in the readout below the chart.

Challenge Questions

1. As doping concentration N increases, what happens to the estimated avalanche breakdown voltage?

Correct. Since VBR ≈ εEcrit²/(2qN), breakdown voltage is inversely proportional to doping — heavier doping means a thinner depletion region that reaches the critical field at a lower voltage.

Look at where N appears in the VBR formula — is it in the numerator or denominator?

2. A diode has a measured breakdown voltage of about 3 V. Which mechanism most likely dominates?

Correct. A breakdown voltage this low requires very heavy doping and a correspondingly thin depletion barrier, characteristic of Zener tunneling rather than avalanche multiplication.

Compare 3 V to the ~6 V crossover region shown on the chart — which regime lies below it?

3. Why does heavy doping favor Zener breakdown over avalanche breakdown?

Correct. Heavier doping shrinks the depletion width W (see the readout below the chart), which both raises the field for a given voltage and shortens the barrier enough for direct quantum tunneling — leaving too little room for the multi-step impact-ionization chain reaction that avalanche breakdown needs.

Look at the depletion-width row in the Zener panel, and the W estimate in the readout below the chart — what happens to the barrier as doping increases?
