<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim draws a schematic band diagram showing the Fermi level's exact position, computed from \(E_C-E_F=k_BT\ln(N_C/n_0)\) using the doping-dependent \(n_0\) from the exact carrier concentration equation. The intrinsic Fermi level \(E_i\) is marked as a dashed reference line, showing its small offset from exact midgap due to \(N_C\neq N_V\).
