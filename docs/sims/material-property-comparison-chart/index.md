<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim compares silicon, germanium, and gallium arsenide across four key properties — band gap, electron mobility, lattice constant, and melting point — using a switchable bar chart. Comparing all four properties in turn shows that no single material dominates every category, motivating why real device engineering involves material trade-offs rather than one universally "best" semiconductor.
</p>
