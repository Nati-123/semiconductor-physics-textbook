    Chapter 1: Physics and Math Foundations

    MicroSim Bloom Level Description
    Coulomb's Law Force Vectors L2 Understand Visualize attractive vs. repulsive force direction between two point charges
    Coulomb's Law Force and Field Explorer L3 Apply Compute force, field, potential, and energy for two adjustable point charges
    Vector Components and Addition L3 Apply Build 2D vectors from magnitude and angle and add them tip-to-tail
    Gradient, Divergence, and Curl Explorer L2 Understand Compare the three vector-calculus operators on preset scalar and vector fields
    Electric Field and Flux Explorer L3 Apply Compute electric flux through a tiltable test surface near a point charge
    Gauss's Law Explorer L3 Apply Show that flux through a Gaussian surface depends only on enclosed charge
    Maxwell-Boltzmann Speed Distribution L2 Understand See how temperature reshapes the Maxwell-Boltzmann speed distribution
    Photon Energy Across the Electromagnetic Spectrum L4 Analyze Compare photon energy to wavelength and locate silicon's band gap

    Chapter 2: Quantum Mechanics Foundations

    MicroSim Bloom Level Description
    Wave-Particle Duality Explorer L2-L3 Understand/Apply Fire single particles at a one- or two-slit barrier and watch an interference fringe pattern build up dot by dot
    de Broglie Wavelength Explorer L3 Apply Compute the de Broglie wavelength of objects from an electron to a bowling ball and compare to atomic and everyday length scales
    Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle Explorer L2-L3 Understand/Apply Visualize the inverse relationship between position and momentum uncertainty for an electron or proton
    Schrödinger Wavefunction & Eigenstates Visualizer L2-L4 Understand/Analyze Animate a complex plane-wave eigenstate and a multi-component Gaussian wave packet, contrasting flat vs. localized-and-spreading probability density
    Particle in a Box Explorer L3-L4 Apply/Analyze Plot the infinite-square-well wavefunction, probability density, and energy-level ladder for n = 1 to 6
    Finite Potential Well Explorer L3-L4 Apply/Analyze Numerically solve for bound-state energies of a finite square well and see the wavefunction penetrate the classically forbidden walls
    Quantum Tunneling Probability Explorer L3 Apply Compute tunneling probability through a rectangular barrier as height and width change

    Chapter 3: Crystal Lattices and Structures

    MicroSim Bloom Level Description
    Unit Cell Repetition Explorer L2 Understand Watch an N×N×N grid of SC, BCC, or FCC unit cells build up cell by cell to see how a single repeating cell tiles into a full crystal
    Crystal Structure Explorer L2-L4 Understand/Apply/Analyze Rotate, pan, and zoom all nine major crystal structures (SC, BCC, FCC, HCP, Diamond Cubic, Zinc Blende, Rock Salt, CsCl, Wurtzite) and compare crystal system, basis, atoms per cell, coordination number, and packing factor
    Diamond / Zincblende Structure Explorer L2 Understand Rotate the diamond-cubic unit cell with tetrahedral bonds, toggling between single-element diamond and two-element zincblende coloring
    Miller Indices Explorer L3 Apply Slice a shaded crystal plane through a rotatable unit cell for adjustable Miller indices (h k l), with presets for (100), (110), (111), and (200)
