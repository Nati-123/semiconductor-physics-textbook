<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots the steady-state solution of the minority carrier continuity equation for carriers injected at \(x=0\) into a long, field-free region: \(\Delta p(x)=\Delta p(0)e^{-x/L_p}\), where \(L_p=\sqrt{D_p\tau_p}\) is the diffusion length. Δp(0), Dp, and τp are all adjustable sliders. A movable position marker reads off the local excess concentration, a dashed reference line marks \(L_p\) on the curve, and an optional panel reveals the diffusion and recombination terms of the continuity equation itself, confirming they balance exactly in steady state.
</p>
