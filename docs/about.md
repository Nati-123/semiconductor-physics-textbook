# About

## About This Book

Semiconductor Physics is an intelligent textbook designed for junior-level Electrical Engineering and Applied Physics undergraduates. It covers the quantum-mechanical and statistical foundations of semiconductor materials and devices, from crystal structure through the p-n junction and MOS capacitor.

Natol Dinka is an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities with a strong interest in semiconductor physics, electronics, integrated circuits, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, and engineering education. This textbook is part of his effort to make semiconductor physics more accessible through interactive learning, combining modern web technologies, intelligent tutoring features, MicroSims, visualizations, quizzes, and concept-based learning.

The Semiconductor Physics Intelligent Textbook is designed to help undergraduate students build a deep conceptual understanding while connecting theory with practical engineering applications. By integrating interactive simulations, worked examples, learning graphs, and AI-assisted educational tools, the project aims to create a more engaging and effective learning experience than traditional textbooks.

This open educational project is continuously developed and improved to support students, educators, and self-learners studying semiconductor physics and modern electronic devices.
