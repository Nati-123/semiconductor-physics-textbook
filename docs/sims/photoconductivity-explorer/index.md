<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim computes the <strong>photoconductivity</strong> increase \(\Delta\sigma=q(\Delta n\mu_n+\Delta p\mu_p)\) of illuminated silicon from generation rate \(G\) and minority carrier lifetime \(\tau\). A log-log chart plots \(\Delta\sigma\) against \(G\) with a live marker, and a schematic photoresistor bar glows brighter as \(\Delta\sigma\) increases.
</p>
