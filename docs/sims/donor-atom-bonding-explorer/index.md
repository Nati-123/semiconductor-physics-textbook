<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim shows a Group V <strong>donor atom</strong> (selectable as phosphorus, arsenic, or antimony) substituted into a silicon lattice. Four of the donor's five valence electrons fill ordinary covalent bonds with its silicon neighbors; the fifth is drawn in a dashed, weakly-bound orbit. Clicking "Ionize Donor" releases this fifth electron as a free carrier and marks the donor atom as a fixed positive ion — without breaking any covalent bond.
</p>
