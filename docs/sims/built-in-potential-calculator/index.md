<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim computes the <strong>built-in potential</strong> \(V_{bi}=(kT/q)\ln(N_AN_D/n_i^2)\) of a p-n junction for a chosen material (silicon, germanium, or gallium arsenide), doping concentrations, and temperature. A toggle switches between two views: <strong>Before Contact</strong>, showing the p-side and n-side as separate, isolated materials each with its own flat Fermi level, and <strong>After Contact (Equilibrium)</strong>, the standard bent-band diagram with a single flat Fermi level — making visually explicit that \(qV_{bi}\) is exactly the gap between the two isolated Fermi levels before contact.
</p>
