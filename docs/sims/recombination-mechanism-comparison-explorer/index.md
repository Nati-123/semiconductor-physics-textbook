<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots three recombination mechanisms as curves against excess carrier concentration Δn on a log-log chart: Shockley-Read-Hall (trap-assisted) recombination, \(R_{SRH}=\Delta n/\tau_{SRH}\); Auger recombination, \(R_{Auger}=C\Delta n^3\); and direct (band-to-band) recombination, \(R_{direct}=B\Delta n(n_0+p_0+\Delta n)\). A movable Δn marker reports which mechanism dominates at that injection level and by how much. A material selector switches between silicon (indirect gap) and GaAs (direct gap), showing how direct recombination's importance depends heavily on band structure.
</p>
