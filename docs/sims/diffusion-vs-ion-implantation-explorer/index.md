<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots the dopant concentration profile produced by <strong>diffusion doping</strong> or <strong>ion implantation</strong>, letting students directly compare a surface-peaked Gaussian diffusion profile to an implantation profile that can be buried at any depth by adjusting its projected range.
</p>
