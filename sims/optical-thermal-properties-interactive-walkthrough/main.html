Challenge Questions

1. Which two devices in this walkthrough are, in a sense, opposite processes of each other?

Correct. A photodiode/solar cell absorbs light and produces current; an LED is driven by current and emits light — the same junction physics run in opposite directions.

Revisit Steps 4-6 — which two devices convert between light and electrical current in opposite directions?

2. Which concept explains why real silicon diodes often leak more current than the ideal diode equation predicts?

Correct. Thermally-generated carriers in the depletion region are swept apart by the field, adding a generation current beyond the ideal diffusion-based saturation current.
