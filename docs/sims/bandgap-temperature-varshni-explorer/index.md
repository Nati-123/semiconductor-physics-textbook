<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim overlays the Varshni-equation band-gap curves, \(E_g(T)=E_g(0)-\alpha T^2/(T+\beta)\), for silicon, germanium, and gallium arsenide on a single chart. Checkboxes isolate individual curves, and a temperature marker slider reads off each visible material's exact band gap at that temperature, making direct numerical and graphical comparison straightforward.
</p>
