<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim computes \(J_n=qn_0\mu_nE\) and \(J_p=qp_0\mu_pE\) in real units (A/cm²) for a chosen material, doping level, temperature, and applied field — combining Chapter 9-10's exact carrier concentration equations with Chapter 11's Matthiessen's-rule mobility model. A log-scale bar chart and a plain-language summary make the majority-carrier dominance the mass action law predicts directly visible, rather than something to take on faith from a single worked example.
</p>
