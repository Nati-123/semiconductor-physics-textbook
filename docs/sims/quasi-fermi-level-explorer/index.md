<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim draws a schematic silicon band diagram showing the electron and hole quasi-Fermi levels, \(E_{Fn}-E_i=k_BT\ln(n/n_i)\) and \(E_i-E_{Fp}=k_BT\ln(p/n_i)\), computed from equilibrium doping plus an injected excess carrier concentration \(\Delta n\). At zero injection the two levels coincide at the single equilibrium Fermi level; as injection increases, they split apart, with the minority carrier's quasi-Fermi level moving the most.
</p>
