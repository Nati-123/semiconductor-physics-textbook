Key Takeaway: Non-equilibrium behavior in a semiconductor is one continuous causal chain, not eight disconnected topics — generation creates excess carriers, recombination removes them at a rate set by the carrier lifetime, the size of the excess relative to doping sets the injection level, the continuity equation tracks the excess in space to give a diffusion length, and quasi-Fermi levels describe how far the carrier populations have been pushed from equilibrium. This walkthrough is the map; the other five MicroSims are where you build intuition for each leg of the journey.
