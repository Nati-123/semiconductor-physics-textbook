<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim shows a GaAs lattice controlled by a Ga-fraction slider centered on perfect 1:1 stoichiometry (0.50). Moving the slider away from 0.50 introduces <strong>antisite defects</strong> — a Ga atom occupying an As site, or vice versa — outlined in red, illustrating a crystal-growth challenge unique to compound semiconductors that elemental semiconductors like silicon never face.
</p>
