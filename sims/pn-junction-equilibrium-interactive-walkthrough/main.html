Key Takeaway: The equilibrium p-n junction is one continuous chain of cause and effect, not eight disconnected facts — joining the two materials forces diffusion, diffusion creates the depletion region, the depletion approximation makes its electrostatics tractable, and Poisson's equation turns the resulting charge density into a field, a potential, a width, and finally a capacitance. Every quantity in Chapter 15's biased junction is a direct modification of something derived here at equilibrium.
