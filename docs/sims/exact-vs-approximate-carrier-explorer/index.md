<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots how far the common shortcut \(n_0\approx N_D-N_A\) diverges from the exact equation \(n_0=\frac{(N_D-N_A)+\sqrt{(N_D-N_A)^2+4n_i^2}}{2}\), as a function of donor concentration on a log scale. A selectable tolerance (1%, 5%, or 10%) shades the region where the approximation is trustworthy, and a live crossover reading reports the smallest doping level where it first meets that tolerance — teaching students not just the formulas, but when they may safely be simplified.
</p>
