<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim uses a single slider, <span class="arithmatex">\(s=\log_{10}(n/n_i)\)</span>, to parametrize doping continuously: electron concentration is <span class="arithmatex">\(n=n_i\cdot10^{s}\)</span> and hole concentration is always <span class="arithmatex">\(p=n_i^2/n\)</span>, so <span class="arithmatex">\(np=n_i^2\)</span> holds exactly at every position. A log-scale bar chart shows both concentrations directly, and a schematic (not-to-scale) population panel shows a mix of electron and hole icons whose proportions shift as doping changes. A badge names the majority carrier, and a predict-then-reveal challenge question asks what happens to hole concentration when electron concentration increases 100×.
</p>
