<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots three mobility curves against temperature: lattice-limited mobility (\(\mu_L\propto T^{-3/2}\)), impurity-limited mobility (\(\mu_I\propto T^{3/2}/N\)), and their Matthiessen's-rule combination, \(1/\mu_{total}=1/\mu_L+1/\mu_I\). Carrier type and doping concentration sliders let students see how each scattering mechanism dominates in different regimes.
</p>
