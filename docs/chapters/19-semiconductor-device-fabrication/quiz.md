<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75;">In the parabolic regime of thermal oxidation, what limits the growth rate?</p>

2. Diffusion of the oxidizing species through the oxide already grown
3. The wafer's doping concentration
4. The photoresist thickness

<summary style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; cursor: pointer;">Show Answer</summary>
<div style="background: #E7F7E7; border: 2px solid #81C784; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px 22px; margin-top: 10px;">
<p style="color: #2E7D32; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 0;">Correct Answer: B</p>
<p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.75; margin-bottom: 0;">As oxide thickens, the oxidizing species must diffuse through more existing oxide to reach the silicon surface, making growth diffusion-limited and giving the parabolic \(x_{ox}\approx\sqrt{Bt}\) dependence.</p>
