<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim has two panels. The top panel computes steady-state temperature rise \(\Delta T=Pt/(\kappa A)\) across a slab for a chosen power and material <strong>thermal conductivity</strong>. The bottom panel computes <strong>thermal generation rate</strong> \(G_{th}=n_i/\tau_0\) and the resulting depletion-region generation current \(I_{gen}=qG_{th}WA\), comparing it on a log-scale bar chart against a fixed reference diffusion-based diode current from Chapter 15.
</p>
