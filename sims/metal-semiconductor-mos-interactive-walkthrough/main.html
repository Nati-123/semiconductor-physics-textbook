Challenge Questions

1. Which concept in this walkthrough directly explains why Schottky diodes switch faster than p-n diodes?

Correct. Because Schottky diodes conduct via majority carriers going over the barrier, there is no minority carrier injection or storage to remove when switching off, unlike a p-n diode.

Revisit Step 5 — what kind of carriers dominate Schottky diode conduction, and does that require minority-carrier storage?

2. At what surface condition does the inversion layer of a MOS capacitor form?

Correct. At ψs = 2φF, the surface electron concentration equals the bulk hole concentration, marking the onset of strong inversion and the formation of the inversion layer.

Revisit Step 9 — which threshold marks the surface electron concentration reaching the bulk doping level?
