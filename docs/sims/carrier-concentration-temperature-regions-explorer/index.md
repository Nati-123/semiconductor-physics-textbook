<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots a doped silicon sample's free carrier concentration against temperature using the simplified combination \(n(T)\approx N_D f_{ion}(T) + n_i(T)\) (or the p-type analog), and shades the plot into three temperature regions — freeze-out, extrinsic, intrinsic — whose boundaries are computed live from the actual doping and ionization-energy sliders, not fixed in place. A movable temperature marker drives a prominent region badge and a plain-language explanation of why the sample is in that region right now, alongside numeric readouts for T, majority/minority carrier concentration, doping level, n_i, and ionized fraction.
</p>
