<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim places a pure silicon lattice side by side with a lattice containing one substitutional donor atom, and pairs each with a realistic, log-scale carrier-concentration bar chart. The intrinsic lattice shows only rare thermally-broken bonds, while the extrinsic lattice shows a free electron contributed immediately by the donor atom — visually explaining why doped (extrinsic) silicon has roughly a million times more carriers than pure (intrinsic) silicon at room temperature.
</p>
