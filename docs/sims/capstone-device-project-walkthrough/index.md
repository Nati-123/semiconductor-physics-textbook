<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim is a complete <strong>capstone device project</strong>: a cascading power diode design calculator. Four specifications — target breakdown voltage, rated current, die area, and die thickness — flow through drift doping and width (Chapters 14-15), specific and absolute on-resistance (Chapter 11), forward voltage drop, power dissipation, and temperature rise (Chapter 17), all shown together as a connected chain that updates live.
</p>
