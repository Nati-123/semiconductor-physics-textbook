Challenge Questions

1. According to step 9, what happens to yield as die area increases at fixed defect density?

Correct. Y = e^(−D0·A) falls off exponentially as die area A increases.

Look at the shape of the yield-vs-area curve in step 9.

2. Which two steps in this timeline are both patterning-and-selective-modification steps applied to a freshly formed layer?

Correct. Photolithography patterns a layer, and etching then selectively removes material through that pattern — the two halves of the manufacturing overview's repeating cycle.
