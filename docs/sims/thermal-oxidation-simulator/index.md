<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots oxide thickness versus <strong>thermal oxidation</strong> time from the Deal-Grove growth law, at a selectable temperature preset. A movable time marker shows how growth starts steep (linear regime) and gradually flattens (parabolic regime) as oxide thickens.
</p>
