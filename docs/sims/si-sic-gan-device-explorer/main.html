Challenge Questions

1. Which material has the highest thermal conductivity?

Correct. SiC's high thermal conductivity is why it is preferred for the highest continuous-power applications.

Select "Thermal Conductivity" from the property dropdown and compare the bars.

2. Which material has the highest typical switching frequency?

Correct. GaN's higher electron mobility supports the fastest switching frequencies among the three.

Select "Typical Switching Frequency" and compare.
