Challenge Questions

1. If breakdown voltage doubles, what happens to specific on-resistance according to this trade-off?

Correct. Since Ron,sp ∝ VBR², doubling VBR quadruples Ron,sp.

Look at the exponent on VBR in the formula shown at the top of the sim.

2. What must happen to drift doping ND as target breakdown voltage increases?

Correct. Since ND ≈ εEcrit²/(2qVBR), higher target breakdown voltage requires lighter (smaller) doping.
