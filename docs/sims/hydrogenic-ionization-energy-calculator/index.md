<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim models a donor's loosely-bound fifth electron as a hydrogen-like system, computing its ionization energy as \(E_D = 13.6\ \text{eV}\times(m^*/m_0)/\varepsilon_r^2\) from adjustable effective-mass-ratio and relative-dielectric-constant sliders. A schematic orbit diagram grows or shrinks alongside the computed energy, and the result is compared directly to real measured donor/acceptor ionization energies in silicon and germanium.
</p>
