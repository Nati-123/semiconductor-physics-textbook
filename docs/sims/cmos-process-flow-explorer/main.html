Challenge Questions

1. Why is the gate patterned before the source/drain implants?

Correct. The gate physically blocks the implant directly beneath it, aligning source and drain edges to the gate automatically.

Watch how the LDD implant region stops exactly at the gate edge in step 3.

2. What is the purpose of the sidewall spacer added in step 4?

Correct. The spacer creates a controlled offset so the heavy source/drain implant lands slightly away from the gate edge, distinct from the lighter LDD extension.

Compare where the LDD implant (step 3) and heavy S/D implant (step 5) each begin relative to the gate.
