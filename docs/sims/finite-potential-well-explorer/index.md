<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim numerically solves for the bound-state energies of a symmetric finite square well of adjustable width <span class="arithmatex">\(L\)</span> and depth <span class="arithmatex">\(V_0\)</span>, using bisection root-finding on the standard even/odd transcendental matching equations. It plots the well, a dashed energy level for every bound state found, and the selected eigenfunction — clearly showing the wavefunction decaying smoothly into the classically forbidden region outside the well, rather than dropping to exactly zero as it does in the idealized infinite well of Particle in a Box.
</p>

<div style="background: #FFF7DD; border: 2px solid #F0D87A; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px 24px; margin: 1.2rem 0;">

<h3 style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.6rem;">Before the Simulation (5 minutes)</h3>

<ul style="list-style: none; padding-left: 0.8rem; margin: 0.5rem 0 1rem 0;">
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;"><span style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-right: 0.5rem;">&#9679;</span> Review the "Finite Potential Wells and Barriers" text and the idea of a classically forbidden region</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;"><span style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-right: 0.5rem;">&#9679;</span> Ask students to predict whether a shallow, narrow well can always support at least one bound state</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.6rem;">During the Simulation (10 minutes)</h3>

<ol style="padding-left: 1.2rem; margin: 0.5rem 0 1rem 0;">
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">Set V₀ to minimum and confirm exactly one bound state exists, with a visibly leaking wavefunction</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">Raise V₀ until 3-4 states appear, and record each state's parity and node count</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">Toggle the infinite-well overlay for the ground state and discuss why the finite-well energy is always somewhat lower</li>
</ol>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;"><span style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-right: 0.5rem;">&#9679;</span> Preview how generalizing a well to a *barrier* (allowed regions on both sides instead of a well) leads directly into quantum tunneling, covered next</li>
