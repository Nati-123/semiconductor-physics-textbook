Challenge Questions

1. According to this chart, what happens to transistor count as process year advances?

Correct. Moore's Law describes exponential transistor-count growth, doubling roughly every two years.

Notice the chart uses a log-scale y-axis — a straight line there corresponds to exponential growth.

2. What happens to the short-channel-effect indicator as gate length shrinks below about 20-30 nm?

Correct. Short-channel effects become severe once gate length becomes comparable to source/drain depletion widths.

Drag the year slider toward 2028-2030 and watch the severity readout.
