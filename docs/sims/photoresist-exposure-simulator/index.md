<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim shows the four stages of <strong>photoresist</strong> patterning — mask, UV exposure, as-exposed resist, and developed pattern — side by side as rows of cells, letting students see exactly how positive and negative resist invert the same mask pattern.
</p>
