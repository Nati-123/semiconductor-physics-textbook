Guided Exploration & Challenge Questions

Try This

  • Try this: Select Wet Etching and note how far the cavity extends sideways under the resist mask.
  • Predict: Before switching to Plasma Etching, predict whether the anisotropy factor will rise or fall.
  • Observe: Plasma etching's near-vertical sidewalls reproduce the mask opening almost exactly, unlike wet etching's rounded undercut.

Connects to Chapter 19: Wet etching is isotropic; dry and especially plasma etching use directional ion bombardment to achieve strong anisotropy, \(A_f=1-R_{lateral}/R_{vertical}\).

What Each Control Does

Etch type
Selects wet, dry, or plasma etching, each with a preset vertical and lateral etch rate.

Challenge Questions

1. Which etch type has the largest lateral undercut relative to its vertical rate?

Correct. Wet etching's isotropic chemical attack removes material sideways almost as fast as downward.

Compare the cavity width under the mask for each preset.

2. An etch with vertical rate 200 nm/min and lateral rate 20 nm/min has an anisotropy factor of:

Correct. Af = 1 − 20/200 = 1 − 0.1 = 0.9.

Use Af = 1 − Rlateral/Rvertical.
