<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots the classic power-device <strong>device design trade-off</strong> between breakdown voltage and specific on-resistance, \(R_{on,sp}\approx4V_{BR}^2/(\mu_n\varepsilon_sE_{crit}^3)\), on a log-log chart. A target breakdown voltage slider shows the required drift doping, drift width, and resulting specific on-resistance together.
</p>
