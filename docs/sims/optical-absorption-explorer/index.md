<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots the Beer-Lambert law, \(I(x)=I_0e^{-\alpha x}\), for a chosen absorption coefficient \(\alpha\), with material presets illustrating the much larger \(\alpha\) of direct-gap GaAs compared to indirect-gap silicon near the band edge. A dashed marker shows the penetration depth \(1/\alpha\).
</p>
