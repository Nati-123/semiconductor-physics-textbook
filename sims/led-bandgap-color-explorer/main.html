Guided Exploration & Challenge Questions

Try This

  • Try this: Sweep band gap from 1.4 eV to 3.4 eV and watch the color swatch move from red through the visible spectrum.
  • Predict: Before dragging past about 3.1 eV, predict whether the emission will remain visible.
  • Observe: Very high band gap pushes emission into the ultraviolet, outside the visible range entirely.

Connects to Chapter 20: Optoelectronic device integration reuses Chapter 17's LED wavelength formula \(\lambda=hc/E_g\) directly — band gap and emitted color are deterministically linked.

What Each Control Does

Band gap
Semiconductor band gap in electron volts, setting emission wavelength and color.

Challenge Questions

1. As band gap increases, what happens to emission wavelength?

Correct. Since λ = hc/Eg, wavelength is inversely proportional to band gap.

Drag the band gap slider higher and watch the λ readout.

2. Which band gap range corresponds to red light?

Correct. Red light has lower photon energy, corresponding to a lower semiconductor band gap.

Drag the slider to its lowest value and observe the swatch color.
