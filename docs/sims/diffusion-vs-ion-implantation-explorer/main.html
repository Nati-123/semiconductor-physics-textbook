Try This

  • Try this: Switch between Diffusion and Ion Implantation and compare where each curve peaks.
  • Predict: Before raising Dt for diffusion, predict whether the profile will spread deeper or stay narrower.
  • Observe: The implantation profile can be placed well below the surface by adjusting projected range, something the diffusion profile can never do.

Connects to Chapter 19: Diffusion doping always peaks at the surface (Gaussian, \(N(x,t)=(Q/\sqrt{\pi Dt})\exp(-x^2/4Dt)\)); ion implantation peaks at a buried projected range \(R_p\) set by ion energy, \(N(x)=(\text{Dose}/\sqrt{2\pi}\Delta R_p)\exp(-(x-R_p)^2/2\Delta R_p^2)\).

What Each Control Does

Method
Selects diffusion or ion implantation doping.
First parameter
For diffusion: the thermal budget Dt. For implantation: projected range Rp.
Second parameter (implantation only)
Implantation straggle ΔRp, controlling profile width.

Challenge Questions

1. Where does the diffusion doping profile always peak?

Correct. The Gaussian diffusion profile N(x,t) always has its maximum at x = 0, decaying monotonically with depth.

Look at where the diffusion curve reaches its highest point on the chart.

2. Why can ion implantation place a profile deep below the surface, but diffusion cannot?

Correct. Momentum-driven ion implantation places dopants directly at a depth controlled by accelerating voltage, independent of any surface source.

Recall the physical placement mechanism described for each doping technique.
