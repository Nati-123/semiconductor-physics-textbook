<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim renders the diamond-cubic conventional unit cell as two interpenetrating Face-Centered Cubic sublattices — one at the corners and face centers, the other offset by <span class="arithmatex">\((\tfrac14,\tfrac14,\tfrac14)a\)</span> along the body diagonal — connected by the four tetrahedral bonds that give every atom coordination number 4. Students click and drag to orbit the structure and toggle between Diamond mode (a single element, such as silicon) and Zincblende mode (two elements, such as gallium and arsenide, distinguished by color).
</p>

<div style="background: #FFF7DD; border: 2px solid #F0D87A; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px 24px; margin: 1.2rem 0;">

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.6rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;"><strong style="color: #5A3EED;">Choose a mode</strong> from the Structure dropdown (Diamond or Zincblende)</li>

<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Lesson Plan</h2>

<h3 style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.6rem;">Before the Simulation (5 minutes)</h3>

<ul style="list-style: none; padding-left: 0.8rem; margin: 0.5rem 0 1rem 0;">
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;"><span style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-right: 0.5rem;">&#9679;</span> Review the Face-Centered Cubic structure from the previous MicroSim and its coordination number of 12</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;"><span style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-right: 0.5rem;">&#9679;</span> Ask students to predict why silicon, a semiconductor, might not use a close-packed structure</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.6rem;">During the Simulation (10 minutes)</h3>

<ol style="padding-left: 1.2rem; margin: 0.5rem 0 1rem 0;">
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">In Diamond mode, rotate the cell and count the 4 bonds leaving one interior atom</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">Switch to Zincblende mode and verify every bond connects a Ga atom (blue) to an As atom (orange), never the same color to itself</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">Compare the atoms-per-cell (8) and coordination number (4) readouts to FCC's (4 and 12) from the previous MicroSim</li>
</ol>

<h3 style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.6rem;">After the Simulation (5 minutes)</h3>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;"><span style="color: #5A3EED; font-weight: 700; margin-right: 0.5rem;">&#9679;</span> Discuss why tetrahedral bonding (sp3 hybridization) favors semiconductors like silicon and GaAs</li>
