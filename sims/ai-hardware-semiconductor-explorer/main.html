Challenge Questions

1. As transistor density increases, what happens to power density relative to compute capability?

Correct. In this simplified model, power density grows faster than linearly with density, outpacing the linear rise in compute capability.

Compare the two bars' heights as you drag the slider toward 100%.

2. Which earlier chapters' physics becomes critical once power density gets very high?

Correct. High power density raises thermal management concerns (Chapter 17) and can affect manufacturing yield (Chapter 19).
