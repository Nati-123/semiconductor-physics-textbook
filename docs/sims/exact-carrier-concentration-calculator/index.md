<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim computes electron and hole concentration exactly, by solving the mass action law and charge neutrality condition together: \(n_0=\big[(N_D-N_A)+\sqrt{(N_D-N_A)^2+4n_i^2}\big]/2\), with \(p_0=n_i^2/n_0\). A charge-balance visualization shows both equations being satisfied simultaneously — a "positive charge" tower (\(p_0+N_D\)) and a "negative charge" tower (\(n_0+N_A\)) that always reach the same height — while the info panel compares the exact \(n_0\) to the common shortcut \(n_0\approx N_D-N_A\) and reports the percent difference. Presets jump directly to intrinsic, n-type, p-type, compensated, and nearly-compensated doping.
</p>
