Guided Exploration & Challenge Questions

Try This

  • Try this: Shorten cantilever length and watch resonant frequency rise.
  • Predict: Before increasing thickness, predict whether resonant frequency will rise or fall.
  • Observe: A shorter, thicker cantilever is much stiffer, producing a much higher resonant frequency than a long, thin one.

Connects to Chapter 20: MEMS and NEMS structures have their resonant frequency, \(f=(1/2\pi)\sqrt{k/m}\), set entirely by the dimensions that Chapter 19's photolithography and etching processes define.

What Each Control Does

Length
Cantilever length in micrometers, defined by photolithography.
Thickness
Cantilever thickness in micrometers, defined by the deposited or etched film thickness.

Challenge Questions

1. Shortening a cantilever's length while holding thickness fixed does what to resonant frequency?

Correct. A shorter cantilever is stiffer (higher k) and has less mass, both raising resonant frequency.

Drag the length slider to its minimum and observe the frequency readout.

2. Which two Chapter 19 fabrication processes most directly set a MEMS cantilever's dimensions?

Correct. Photolithography defines the lateral pattern and plasma etching releases the free-standing structure.

Recall which two processes pattern and release a free-standing structure in Chapter 19.
