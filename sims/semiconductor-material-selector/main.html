Challenge Questions

1. Why is GaAs, not silicon, recommended for LEDs and laser diodes?

GaAs's direct band gap allows efficient photon-only emission at the band edge, while silicon's indirect gap requires a much less probable phonon-assisted process, as covered in Chapter 6.
