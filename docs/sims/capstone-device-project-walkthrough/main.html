Connects to Chapter 18: This capstone device project chains together the avalanche breakdown formula (Chapter 15), the depletion width formula (Chapter 14), drift-region resistance (Chapter 11), and the thermal conductivity formula (Chapter 17) into one complete, connected power diode design — exactly the kind of multi-chapter synthesis this course's capstone learning outcome calls for.

What Each Control Does

Target V_BR
Sets drift doping and width (Chapters 14-15), which set specific on-resistance.
Rated I
Determines the drift-region ohmic voltage drop and total power dissipation.
Die area A
Converts specific on-resistance to absolute resistance, and enters the thermal calculation.
Die thickness
Enters the temperature-rise calculation directly.

Challenge Questions

1. Which two quantities depend only on target breakdown voltage, not on current, area, or thickness?

Correct. ND and W are set purely by the blocking-voltage requirement (Chapters 14-15) — they don't depend on the current, area, or thickness sliders at all.

Try changing only the current slider — which boxes stay exactly the same?

2. If you increase die area A while keeping everything else fixed, what happens to Ron and VF?

Correct. Ron = Ron,sp/A decreases as A increases, which directly lowers the drift voltage drop and total VF — a larger die trades cost/size for lower forward drop.

Recall that Ron,sp is fixed once V_BR is fixed — what happens to Ron = Ron,sp/A as A grows?
