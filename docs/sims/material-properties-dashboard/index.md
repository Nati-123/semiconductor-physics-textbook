<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim is a property dashboard for silicon, germanium, and gallium arsenide: selecting a material loads its crystal structure, band gap type, lattice constant, melting point, and electron mobility onto a property card, while a temperature slider drives a live Varshni-equation band-gap calculation, \(E_g(T)=E_g(0)-\alpha T^2/(T+\beta)\), shown both numerically and as a marker on the material's Eg-vs-T curve.
</p>
