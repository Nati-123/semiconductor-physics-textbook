Challenge Questions

1. Which structure has the shortest relative natural length?

Correct. With n=4 gate-controlled sides, GAA has the shortest natural length and strongest short-channel-effect suppression.

Compare the λ readout for each structure — more gate sides means a smaller n in the denominator.

2. Why does natural length depend on 1/√n rather than 1/n?

Correct. The natural-length formula's square-root dependence on n comes directly from solving the relevant electrostatics for multi-gate structures.
