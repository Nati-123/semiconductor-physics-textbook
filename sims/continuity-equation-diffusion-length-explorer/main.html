Try This

  • Try this: Drag the position marker along the curve and watch Δp(x) fall off exponentially as you move away from the injection point at x=0.
  • Predict: Before increasing τp, predict whether the diffusion length Lp will grow or shrink.
  • Observe: Increasing either Dp or τp stretches the curve outward and moves the dashed Lp line to the right, since \(L_p=\sqrt{D_p\tau_p}\).

Connects to Chapter 13: The continuity equation governs how excess carrier concentration evolves in space and time under diffusion, drift, generation, and recombination. Its steady-state solution for carriers injected at one point and diffusing into a long field-free region is a decaying exponential, characterized by the diffusion length \(L_p=\sqrt{D_p\tau_p}\) — the average distance a minority carrier diffuses before recombining, and this exponential curve is exactly the steady-state carrier profile referenced throughout later device chapters.

What Each Control Does

Dp (diffusion coefficient)
Sets how quickly holes spread by diffusion, stretching the profile and Lp.
τp (lifetime)
Sets how long holes survive before recombining, also stretching the profile and Lp.
Position marker
Reads off the excess hole concentration at a chosen distance from the injection point.
