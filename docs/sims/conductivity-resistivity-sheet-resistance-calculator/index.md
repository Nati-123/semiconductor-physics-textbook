<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim computes conductivity (\(\sigma=qN\mu\)), resistivity (\(\rho=1/\sigma\)), sheet resistance (\(R_s=\rho/t\)), and total resistance (\(R=R_s\times L/W\)) for a doped semiconductor film, using the same Matthiessen's-rule mobility model as the mobility explorer. A resistor diagram divided into unit squares makes the "number of squares" concept behind sheet resistance concrete.
</p>
