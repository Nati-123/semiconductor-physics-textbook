Challenge Questions

1. Which material has the highest electron mobility?

Correct. GaAs has the highest electron mobility of the three, favoring high-frequency electronics.

Compare the mobility bar heights across all three materials.

2. Is silicon a direct-gap or indirect-gap material, unlike GaAs and InP?

Correct. Silicon is indirect-gap (Chapter 6), which is part of why silicon LEDs are inefficient compared to direct-gap GaAs or InP devices.

Select Silicon and read the band gap type field.
