<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim zooms in on a small 5&times;5 patch of an otherwise-perfect lattice and shows exactly one of the chapter's three point defect types at a time, selected from a dropdown: a <strong>vacancy</strong> (a missing atom, marked with a dashed red outline), an <strong>interstitial</strong> (an extra atom squeezed between regular sites, shown in orange and visibly off the grid lines), or a <strong>substitutional</strong> defect (a foreign atom, labeled "P" for phosphorus, occupying a regular site in place of the host atom). Each selection updates an explanation panel describing exactly how that defect disrupts the perfect lattice.
</p>
