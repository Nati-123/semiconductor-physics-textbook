Challenge Questions

1. Which deposition method gives the best sidewall conformality inside a deep, narrow trench?

Correct. ALD's sequential, self-limiting surface reactions coat every exposed surface, including the sidewalls, with equal thickness.

Run the animation for each method and compare the sidewall thickness in the trench.

2. Why does PVD leave the trench sidewalls thin or bare?

Correct. Physically ejected atoms travel in straight lines, so they mostly land on horizontal surfaces directly in their path, missing shadowed sidewalls.

Think about the physical mechanism: are PVD atoms chemically reacting, or simply flying in from a source?
