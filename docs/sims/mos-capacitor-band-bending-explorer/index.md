<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim draws a gate / oxide / p-type semiconductor band diagram, with the semiconductor's conduction and valence bands bending at the surface according to a surface-potential slider. The regime label (accumulation, flat-band, depletion, weak inversion, strong inversion) updates live based on the doping-dependent thresholds \(\phi_F\) and \(2\phi_F\).
</p>
