<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim compares excess carrier concentration \(\Delta n\) to doping concentration \(N\) on side-by-side log-scale bars, classifying the result as low-level injection (\(\Delta n\ll N\)) or high-level injection (\(\Delta n\) comparable to or exceeding \(N\)), with the classification and ratio updating live as either slider moves.
</p>
