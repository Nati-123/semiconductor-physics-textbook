<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim plots the real, physically smooth transition of electron and hole concentration \(n(x)\), \(p(x)\) across a silicon p-n junction (solid curves) directly against the idealized, abrupt <strong>depletion approximation</strong> (dashed curves) used throughout this chapter. Three shaded bands label the <strong>quasi-neutral p-region</strong>, the <strong>depletion region</strong>, and the <strong>quasi-neutral n-region</strong>, and a thin strip below the chart shows the fixed ionized dopant charge the approximation assumes is exposed only inside the depletion edges \(-x_p\) and \(x_n\).
</p>
