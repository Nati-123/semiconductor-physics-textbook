Connects to Chapter 7: Silicon, germanium, and gallium arsenide are the three semiconductor materials most widely used in practice, and each has a distinct profile of band gap, electron mobility, and lattice constant. No material dominates every category, which is exactly why device engineers choose a specific material for a specific application rather than always using the same one.

Challenge Questions

1. Which material has the largest band gap at room temperature?

GaAs has the largest room-temperature band gap of the three, about 1.42 eV, compared to silicon's 1.12 eV and germanium's 0.66 eV.

Select "Band Gap (eV)" from the dropdown and compare bar heights.

2. Which material has the highest electron mobility, making it attractive for high-frequency devices?

GaAs has the highest electron mobility of the three (~8500 cm²/V·s), which is why it is favored for high-frequency RF and microwave devices.

Select "Electron Mobility" from the dropdown and compare bar heights.
