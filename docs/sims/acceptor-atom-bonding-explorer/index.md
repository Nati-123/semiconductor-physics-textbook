<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim shows a Group III <strong>acceptor atom</strong> (selectable as boron, aluminum, gallium, or indium) substituted into a silicon lattice. Only 3 of the 4 required covalent bonds can be completed, leaving one bond incomplete — a hole. Clicking "Ionize Acceptor" shows a neighboring electron filling that vacancy, effectively moving the hole away and leaving the acceptor atom as a fixed negative ion.
</p>
