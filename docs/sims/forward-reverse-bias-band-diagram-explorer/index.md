<h2 style="color: #5A3EED !important; border-bottom: 2px solid #5A3EED; padding-bottom: 0.3rem; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 2rem;">Description</h2>

<p style="color: #555; line-height: 1.85; font-size: 1.02rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
This MicroSim shows how an applied bias \(V\) modifies the equilibrium band diagram of a silicon p-n junction. A live band diagram bends according to the net barrier height \(V_{bi}-V\), and a results card reports the barrier height and depletion width \(W(V)\) as the bias slider moves between reverse and forward bias.
</p>
