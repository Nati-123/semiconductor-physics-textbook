  • Predict: Before selecting GaN, predict whether its on-resistance will be lower or higher than SiC's.

Challenge Questions

1. Which material has the lowest specific on-resistance at a fixed high breakdown voltage?

Correct. GaN's highest critical field among the three drives the lowest specific on-resistance at a given breakdown voltage.

Compare the R_on,sp readout across all three materials at the same V_BR.

2. Why does critical field matter more than mobility in this formula?

Correct. R_on,sp ∝ 1/(μn·Ecrit³) — the cubed critical-field term dominates over the linear mobility term.
