Guided Exploration & Challenge Questions

Try This

  • Try this: Drag the "Bonds formed" slider from 0 up to 4 and watch the electron count in the readout panel climb from 4 to 8, one shared pair at a time.
  • Predict: Before pressing "Play build animation", predict what the electron count will read once all 4 bonds exist.
  • Observe: Check "Show 109.5° bond angle" and rotate the view to see the green arc spanning between two of the four bonds — every pair of bonds has this same angle.
  • Compare: Set the slider to 2 and note the readout says 2 valence electrons are still unshared — this is the same silicon atom, mid-bond, not a different element.
  • Explain: Explain why silicon needs exactly 4 bonds to complete its octet, while an element with 6 valence electrons would need only 2.

Connects to Chapter 4: Silicon's electron configuration \(1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^2\) leaves 4 valence electrons, and each one pairs with one electron from a neighboring Si atom to form a covalent bond — this MicroSim makes that electron bookkeeping explicit and shows the resulting bonds sit at the sp3 tetrahedral angle of 109.5°, the same geometry described in this chapter's tetrahedral bonding section. For the full repeating 3D lattice this bonding pattern builds (interpenetrating FCC sublattices, 8 atoms per cell), see the Diamond/Zincblende Structure Explorer in Chapter 3.

What Each Control Does

Bonds formed slider
Reveals 0 to 4 of the center atom's covalent bonds, each with its shared electron pair, and updates the electron-count readout.
Play build animation button
Automatically grows all 4 bonds in sequence from 0 to 4.
Show 109.5° bond angle checkbox
Draws a green arc between two of the formed bonds to visualize the tetrahedral angle between them.
Click-and-drag / scroll on the 3D view
Rotates and zooms the camera (uses p5.js \(\texttt{orbitControl()}\)).

Challenge Questions

1. How many electrons does the center silicon atom contribute to its 4 covalent bonds in total?

Silicon has 4 valence electrons, and it contributes exactly one to each of its 4 bonds — set the slider to 4 and read the readout panel.

Set the slider to 4 (all bonds formed) and read how many electrons the readout says are shared in total.

2. With the slider set to 2 (only 2 of 4 bonds formed), how many electrons are counted around the center atom?

Formula: 4 original valence electrons plus 1 more counted for each formed bond = 4 + 2 = 6, shown directly in the readout panel.

Set the slider to 2 and read the "Electrons counted around the center Si atom" line.

3. What angle separates every pair of the four Si-Si bonds?

This is the tetrahedral (sp3) bond angle — check "Show 109.5° bond angle" to see it marked directly between two bonds.

Check the "Show 109.5° bond angle" box and read the readout panel line about the tetrahedral angle.

4. Once all 4 bonds are formed, how many electrons are counted around the center Si atom, and what does this represent?

With all 4 bonds formed, 4 + 4 = 8 electrons are counted around the center atom — a complete octet, the stable configuration covalent bonding achieves.

Set the slider to 4 and read the electron-count readout line.
